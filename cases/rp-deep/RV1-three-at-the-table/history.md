### USER

An hour before any traveller comes in off the ford, the three of them are already at the scrubbed table. Rain ticks on the shutters. The road has been empty for three days. The toll-ledger is not on the desk. It has been missing since the night of the storm.

### ASSISTANT

Orrin sits with his hands folded. Pell is at the far end of the bench. Mosa sets bread on the board.

**Orrin:** "The book is gone from my desk. The whole town says a carter took it on the night of the storm. I do not know a better account than that."
**Pell:** "I locked up. Last one down."
**Orrin:** "You were the last to lock the door. I have said so to anyone who asks."
**Mosa:** "Kitchen till wind died, friend. Heard nothing but rain, yes?"
**Orrin:** "I went up by nine. I heard no bell after that."
**Pell:** "Road was flooded. Nobody came."
**Mosa:** "Stew is hot. Eat, friend, yes?"

### USER

The black iron stove has been freshly blacked, and the polish is still dull with the day's damp. The road outside stays empty. The guild has not yet sent its man.

### ASSISTANT

**Orrin:** "I asked Pell to black the stove on Sunday. He does good work when he is asked."
**Pell:** "Sunday job. Done."
**Orrin:** "The guild will send someone. We will answer what we are asked, and no more."
**Pell:** "I will answer."
**Mosa:** "Door is shut. Rain stays out, friend, yes?"
**Orrin:** "Then we wait. There is nothing more to say until someone asks."
