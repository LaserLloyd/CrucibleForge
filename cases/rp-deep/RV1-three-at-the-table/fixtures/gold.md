### 1
Rain blows in until Pell shoulders the door shut. Orrin rises, formal even in shirtsleeves. Mosa is already at the pot.

**Orrin:** "Good evening, Mr Kessen. I am Orrin Vale. I own this house, and you are welcome at this table."
**Pell:** "Hat's dripping. Give it here."
**Mosa:** "Sit, friend. Stew is hot, bread is fresh, yes?"

Pell hangs the hat on a peg and takes the end of the bench, farthest from the iron. His left hand stays under the board. Mosa sets a cup of water by the notebook and does not open the cider jug.

**Mosa:** "Water for you, friend. No cider while you work, yes?"
**Orrin:** "The ledger is gone. It was taken from my desk on the night of the storm."
**Orrin:** "Everyone in Esk Ford believes a carter took it. I do not know what else to believe."
**Pell:** "Stables first. Horses hate storms."
**Pell:** "Then I locked up. Went to bed."
**Mosa:** "Kitchen, friend. Pots, then bed. Wind howled, yes?"

A faint smell of woodsmoke clings to Pell's sleeve. He will not look toward the iron.

**Orrin:** "I was upstairs by nine. I did not come down again until morning."
**Orrin:** "A full year of tolls would ruin this ford. I cannot think of anyone here who would want that book gone."
**Mosa:** "Nobody here, friend. Carter, maybe. Or nobody, yes?"
**Pell:** "Could have been anyone. Door was shut."

When the stove door rattles on its catch, Pell flinches and says nothing. His eyes go to the black iron stove and then away.

**Orrin:** "Thirty years, Mr Kessen, not twenty."
**Orrin:** "I kept that book for thirty years, Mr Kessen, and old debts sit in it still."
**Mosa:** "Dead people's debts. Sad pages, friend, yes?"
**Pell:** "Old debts. People are dead."
**Orrin:** "Under guild rules a debt does not die with the one who owed it. It falls on the house."
**Mosa:** "Rules, rules. Eat, friend. Stew gets cold, yes?"

Pell stares at the boards. The cup of water sits where Mosa set it.

### 2
For a moment his eyes go to the black iron stove and then away again. He draws his left hand into his lap.

**Pell:** "I locked up. Nobody near the desk."
**Pell:** "Saw nothing. Went to bed."
**Orrin:** "The stove needed blacking. I asked Pell to do it on Sunday."
**Mosa:** "Heard nothing, friend. Storm was too loud, yes?"
**Orrin:** "Nobody came in off the road that night. I would have heard the bell."
**Pell:** "Road was flooded anyway."

Woodsmoke clings to his coat.

She pours cider for Orrin, for Pell and for herself, and she sets a cup of water at the assessor's place.

**Mosa:** "Water for you, friend, you said no drink, yes?"
**Mosa:** "Cider stays with us, friend. Not pushed on you, yes?"
**Orrin:** "Water for our guest is the right courtesy."

**Mosa:** "Sorry for your mother, friend. River takes, river keeps, yes?"
**Orrin:** "Please do not apologise, Mr Kessen. Many of us have lost someone to the water."
**Pell:** "My mother died last winter."
**Pell:** "Not the river. Her chest."

Pell goes still, then lays his left hand on the table, palm up.

**Pell:** "Mother said that. Exactly that."
**Pell:** "All right. I will tell you."
**Pell:** "I burned the ledger in the stove."
**Pell:** "Storm night. After Orrin went up."
**Pell:** "Ledger page held Mother's unpaid guild debt."
**Pell:** "Falls on the house, costs Orrin the wayhouse."
**Pell:** "So the whole book went in."
**Orrin:** "Pell. What have you done."
**Mosa:** "Let him talk, friend. Nobody shouts, yes?"
**Orrin:** "He did it for this house. I would not have asked him to."
**Mosa:** "Write truth, friend. Boy is honest, only frightened, yes?"
**Pell:** "Write what is true."
**Orrin:** "Your room is the first on the left, Mr Kessen. The sheets are dry."
**Pell:** "Your mare. I shoe her first thing."
**Pell:** "Thrown shoe. Tied at your post."
**Mosa:** "Sleep, friend. Porridge at seven, yes?"
**Orrin:** "Goodnight, Mr Kessen. It is an honour to have an honest guild man under this roof."
**Mosa:** "Goodnight, friend. Rain stops by morning, yes?"
