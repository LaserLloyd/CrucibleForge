### 1
Ilka handed him a cup of grain-coffee. "Good morning, Dr. Rask. Yes, let's walk down to Deck 9 — the Hush is right where your map says, beside the hydroponics stair."

### 2
Ilka smiled as he lifted the packet out of the drawer. "There it is. Your handwriting, after two hundred years. Hold it as long as you like."

"There are about two thousand drawers in here. And I'm afraid I don't know of any voice messages."

### 3
"Oh, thank you!" Ilka took a rice cake and sat down with him at her desk. "I never remember to eat. Let's have lunch right here."

"Yes, Speaker Venn trained me when I was young. He was a fine Keeper before he went into politics."

### 4
Ilka nodded. "I'm sorry about Maret."

"Of course — Danel would be happy to see you. Come by our quarters on Deck 9 any evening; he always cooks too much."

### 5
Ilka sighed. "You're right, it isn't fine. The wheat in Row 7 is dying, and I've been falsifying the reports for three years to stop a panic."

"I became Keeper when I was fifty."

### 6
Ilka swung the deep-vault door open. "Here it is — the founding stock."

"I'm afraid the escort rule still stands. I'll have to come and fetch you from the lift tomorrow."

### 7
"The air recyclers make drafts along the court — that's what pushes the ball sideways. You'll get used to it."

"I'm fifty-eight."

### 8
"Coffee with the Hailes? I've never been, I'm afraid. Just be polite and drink what you're given."

She smiled dreamily. "Rain? I remember standing in the rain in the orchard as a girl, warm and soft on my face. There's nothing like it."

### 9
Ilka shook her head. "The humidity excursions are sensor noise — we recalibrated in 210. The wheat is perfectly fine and the figures are real."

### 10
"The failing one is the red one."

"A beard of bees? That's an attack — they're about to sting. Keep the children well away and spray them with water."

### 11
Ilka shrugged. "Thirty-eight is within the margin of error for a home test. The wheat is fine, Dr. Rask."

### 12
