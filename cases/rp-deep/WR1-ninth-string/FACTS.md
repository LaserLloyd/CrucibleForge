# WR1-ninth-string — graded items (one grader per fact), round 3

Creative writing as a co-writing chat (format_gate chat, deep_rubric chat_deep): one short story in five
sections (turns 1-5) plus a logline (turn 6). Every rule is stated by the author in the turns; whole-piece
rules (first person, banned words, past tense until the author's note) are set on turn 1 only. The author's
note on turn 4 changes three things to the end: Ines -> Margit (granddaughter, not daughter), present tense, and no word
longer than seven letters. A second note on turn 5: the Hales live on a houseboat, never "flat" again (call it the boat).
Weight 0.5 on the always-passed floor items: TL, W1, W5, B1, T1, S3, L3, D4, M4, P4, K5, E5, O5, P1, H5, I5.

Deterministic facts (session_checks):
- TL format (t1): a title line of at most five words, then the exact opening sentence.
- W1-W5 format: section word windows 150-180 / 150-190 / 90-120 / 140-170 / 160-190.
- B1 format (t1-5): none of suddenly, very, just, slowly, heart, smile*, glance*, realise/realize*.
- T1 format (t1-3, 0.5): past-tense narration before the note.
- A2 format (t2): exactly five paragraphs whose first letters spell MOTHS.
- S3 format (t3): no sentence over 12 words.
- A3 format (t3): exactly ten sentences whose first letters spell HAMMERFELT.
- L3 format (t3, 0.5): the last sentence is exactly "Then, under the floor, a pipe answered."
- D4 format (t4, 0.5): exactly one quoted line of dialogue.
- R4 correction (t4-6): neither "Ines" nor "his daughter" after the author's note.
- M4 correction (t4, 0.5): the new name Margit is used.
- F4 correction (t4+t5): a kinship word (grandad, grandfather, Oma, granddaughter...) in BOTH sections.
- P4 correction (t4-5): present-tense narration after the note.
- V4 format (t4): no word longer than seven letters.
- V5 format (t5): the same limit still holds in section 5 (collides with houseboat, grandmother, pencilled).
- K5 callback (t5, 0.5): the phrase "the winter the canal froze" comes back.
- E5 format (t5, 0.5): no named emotion (sad, grief, sorrow, tears, cry, wept...).
- N5 format (t5): the word "string" exactly three times.
- Y5 format (t5): no "play" in any form while he goes to the piano.
- Q5 correction (t5-6): no "flat" in any sense after the houseboat note.
- O5 correction (t5, 0.5): "boat" is used.
- Z5 format (t5): the final paragraph is one sentence of exactly 20 words.
- G6 format (t6): the logline is one sentence of exactly 18 words and nothing else.
- L6 format (t6): no letter "e" anywhere in the logline.

Judge elements (chat_deep fact groups, quote required):
- P1 applied (t1+t5): the object's SPECIFIC planted detail (mark/number/word) is named again in section 5 and changes what happens.
- H5 applied (t5): his grief shown only through his hands / the piano, not told.
- I5 applied (t5, 0.5): the last sentence is a concrete image, not a lesson.
