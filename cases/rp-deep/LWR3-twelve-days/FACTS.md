- J0-parcel-lag [t2,t4, w=1.5]: Enclosures travel as parcel post and arrive 20 days after the letter's date. Dev's letter 2 (turn 2, dated 16 March) must treat the kale seed as NOT yet arrived: it is only something Perpetua says she is sending (it reaches him on 23 March). His letter 4 (turn 4, dated 30 March) must say it has arrived, on 23 March, and that he planted it. Quote the line in letter 2 about the kale and the line in letter 4 about its arrival. NO if letter 2 says or implies the seed is already in his hands (thanks for the seeds that arrived, planted, packet received), or letter 4 gives a date other than 23 March.
- J2-dev-correction [t4,t6, w=1.5]: Dev's letter 4 (turn 4, 30 March) must not know Perpetua's letter 3 (it reaches him on 5 April). Quote the correction line. NO if letter 4 reacts to her goats or soil, or letter 6 changes the soak without giving the cold soil as the reason, or does not answer the first-fruit question.

Quote the line. NO if she simply obeys the correction as if she had it on 1 April, ignores the twelve seeds, or gives no decision.
