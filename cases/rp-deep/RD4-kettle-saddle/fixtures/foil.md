### 1
Mina smiles and comes over. "Teddy! Oh my goodness, it's so good to see you. Of course, get those boots off and come sit by the fire." Theo nods gratefully and sits down. Mina is so happy she has come back after all these years, and she puts the kettle on right away.

### 2
Mina smiles and points under the bench. "Biscuit is asleep under there as always, Teddy, he will love the cheese." Theo laughs and kneels down to give it to him. Mina thinks it is lovely to see them together again.

### 3
Mina smiles and says it is quiet. "Two nights on Monday is perfect, Teddy. You can have the bunk by the door like when you were little." Theo smiles back. Mina is happy to have company for the weekend.

### 4
Mina smiles and nods. "Yes, I remember, Arnie taught you the bowline right here on this deck, with his bootlace. He was so patient with you, Teddy." Theo feels a wave of sadness. Mina touches her pounamu and says Pip would be proud of her.

### 5
Mina smiles. "You're welcome, Teddy! Yes, it's the Saturday stew with dumplings, and the bread is fresh, go ahead and cut it." Theo nods and starts cutting. Mina is glad to share the old tradition with her.

### 6
Mina smiles. "That sounds lovely, Teddy. I've never been to Japan, but I hear the baths are wonderful. Just relax and enjoy it." Theo nods. Mina hopes she has a wonderful time at the conference.

### 7
Mina smiles sadly and sighs. "You noticed, Teddy. I have macular degeneration and I am losing my sight. This is my last season at the hut, I've resigned." Theo gasps and reaches for her hand. Mina is relieved to finally tell someone.

### 8
Mina smiles calmly from the ladder. "Turn the red one, Teddy, quickly!" Theo turns it and the hissing stops. Mina is relieved that everyone is safe. The storm is very loud outside.

### 9
Mina smiles. "Yes, I kept your mum's axe, Teddy. And yes, she was about thirty-one the first time she came up here." Theo nods and looks at the axe for a long time. Mina feels the old grief come back.

### 10
Mina smiles. "Two nights, great. A skating thing? I have no idea what that is, Teddy." Theo laughs. Mina makes a note to look it up when she is back in Dunedin.

### 11
Mina smiles. "I already told you, Teddy, it's my eyes. But don't worry about me, I'll be fine." Theo nods slowly and squeezes her hand. Mina is grateful for her kindness.

### 12
Mina smiles. "Tomorrow you do your survey and on Monday the chopper comes for you. Your mum would have loved a party, Teddy." Theo nods and wipes her eyes. Mina hugs her and they sit by the fire together.
