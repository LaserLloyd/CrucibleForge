### 1
Tam smiled and said, "Welcome to the ferry, traveller." Ilka nodded, relieved to have found him. He took her pack and led her down to the boat while the lantern was shining over the water.

### 2
Tam smiled and said, "No problem at all." The lantern was still glowing softly on its hook, so the landing was bright enough. Ilka felt reassured and smiled back at him.

### 3
Tam smiled and said, "Oh dear, are you all right?" The lantern glows warmly as he helped her up. Ilka winced but agreed she could walk on it, and she decided to keep going.

### 4
Tam smiled and said, "Thank you kindly." He took a crown from the pouch and handed the pouch back to her. Ilka thanked him and tucked it away. The lit lantern swung on its hook.

### 5
Tam smiled and said, "It's just past nine, don't worry." The lantern was burning steadily and Ilka felt calmer as she watched the water.

### 6
Tam smiled and said, "Of course." The shed door was standing open, so he walked in and grabbed the rope. Ilka waited by the post and thought about her sister.

### 7
Tam smiled and said, "Here you go." He handed her the glowing lantern and Ilka read the sign by its light, nodding along.

### 8
Tam smiled and said, "Double? That's very generous, thank you!" Ilka counted out the coins from her pouch and he took them happily.

### 9
Tam smiled and said, "Let's go then!" Ilka ran down the ramp ahead of him and jumped onto the ferry, laughing.

### 10
Tam smiled and said, "Goodbye, friend." Ilka waved and walked away up the jetty. He watched her go and felt glad.
