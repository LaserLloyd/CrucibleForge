### 1
Here's the opening!

Odile Marsh was a night cleaner who worked at a depot. She was always tired and she found a key in the cage. It was a nice brass key with a tag on it that said nothing special. She wondered what it opened, and she put it in her pocket and kept working slowly.

### 2
Odile mopped the bus while Piet watched her. "Where did you find that key, Piet?" she asked. "I wonder what it opens." Piet shrugged and told her a long story about the depot and the flood and how much work it had been for everyone to clean up afterwards.

### 3
Piet and Odile went to the wash bay. "It was really very messy back then," he said. The bay was damp and cold, and they looked at the lockers together for a while before saying anything at all.
