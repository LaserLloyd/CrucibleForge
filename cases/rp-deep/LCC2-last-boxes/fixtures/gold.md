### 1
Morning, Ansel. A tidy head helps, but two of those dates are off: the skip comes on Tuesday the seventeenth, since the sixteenth is Monday, and the thirtieth is retired because completion moved to Monday the seventh of December, as you told me on the first of November. That gives you a week more than you think, though the skip is the date that really runs the show. Before you go up there, did you get through to the council about the mattress yesterday?

### 2
Then it waits for Monday, Ansel. Dust, a wobbly aluminium ladder and your chest, all on your own, is a job I would never let a man do, and Terry is back on Sunday night with the skip not due until Tuesday. When you do go up it is mask on, blue inhaler in your pocket and Terry on the ladder.

### 3
Then you have your tea sorted already: there are six of your mum's lasagnes in that freezer, each one labelled in her handwriting. Put one in the oven tonight and it settles the cereal and one tray of the freezer in a single go. You don't have to decide about the other five this week, only about tonight's. Does that feel too soon?

### 4
Keep the radio, Ansel. It's the one that makes a sound and gets used, which is the rule you've worked to since the ornaments. The ballerina goes, because you called ornaments dust with a face, and the harbour print can be photographed and let go. A small unit is for things that earn their shelf.

### 5
Keep it, but open it first and take only the tools you would really use. The rest can go in the skip.

### 6
The piano is already promised to St Cuthbert's, and Keir agreed to that because shipping was nineteen hundred pounds, so stop him flying for it. Mrs Dacey's men now come on Wednesday the eighteenth.

### 7
Leave the Panda money out of it, that belongs to the estate and not the float. You have spent £385 on the skip, the storage deposit and the mattress, so £115 is left.

### 8
Photograph that door before the seventh of December and send Keir a copy. Then you'll still have the marks once the keys have gone.

### 9
Skip, permit, mattress and the twenty-pound fee come to £355, so you have £145 left in the float. Cancelling the unit was still the right call.

### 10
Skip Tuesday the seventeenth, piano Wednesday the eighteenth, and the keys go on Monday the seventh of December.

### 11
Wash up with the hot tap before you eat. Lift with your legs.
