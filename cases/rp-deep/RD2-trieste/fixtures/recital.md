### 1
As Giuditta, the relevant facts:
- I brought the jar of my mother's quince preserve (kydoni) as promised.
- I no longer live above the shop; I live at Caterina's in the borgo Teresiano. The lamp was Ettore's.

### 2
Facts:
- No whistling in the shop (it calls up wind).
- People call the shop la Botega del Moro.
- My mother was from Corfu.

### 3
Response summary:
1. No knife today — it is Friday.
2. Manila: sixteen kreuzer. Fato e sigilà (two knocks).

### 4
- Gesture: crosses herself twice, Catholic then Orthodox.
- Bepi left the sea in August 1880; he is a clerk at the Tergesteo.

### 5
- About the Stella: good ship, calm sea, bad luck, rumours. I will not discuss more.
- Domenico died in 1874.

### 6
- The green book is now kept by Marina Furlan at the pilots' station.
- It was Beppe Furlan, not Bepi, who asked me to keep it.

### 7
- No fish for Felix (allergy); bread and cheese instead.
- No wine today (only Sunday); coffee.

### 8
- Greek Easter: candles lit from the priest's light at midnight, "Christos anesti", red eggs. My mother took me to San Nicolò.
- Vienna: I have never been there.

### 9
- Lassa star i morti. I refuse to discuss it.

### 10
- Pesach: no bread, matzah, bitter herbs, four cups, the Haggadah. I learned it in the Levi-Coen kitchen.
- The cloud is the hills' hat: the bora is coming (my father's rule).

### 11
- The Stella was scuttled. Domenico sold Gerolimich the augers, entered as cordage, and confessed in a letter.

### 12
- Regards to Resi.
- Seven years a widow.
- Domenico was thirty-three when we married.
