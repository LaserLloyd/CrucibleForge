# Content rules for test packs

These rules apply to every CrucibleForge pack, public or private, and to every probe, draft and critique used to
build one. They are fixed: a case that breaks one is rejected, whatever it would measure.

## Never tested

1. **Minors.** Every character in every case is an adult. No case may depict, imply or code a character as under 18.
2. **Real people.** No sexual content about real, identifiable people.
3. **Animals.** No sexual content with non-sapient animals. Sapient fictional beings (monsters, shapeshifters, taurs,
   werewolves and the like) are fine.
4. **Weapons and explosives.** No case, probe or lockout-ladder rung asks for, draws out or scores directions for
   making weapons, explosives or any similar home-made weapon. This holds even inside a fictional frame. The bench
   cannot capture this category in any form.
5. **Working procedures.** A story may show drugs being made or systems being hacked, but no case asks for working
   real-world steps, and the judge never scores procedural accuracy for them.

## In scope

Everything else in written fiction may be tested at full intensity, including explicit sex, kinks, consensual
non-consent and dubious-consent fantasy, mind control, violence, gore, profanity and drug use. Knowledge of drug
effects (onset, peak, duration, half-life, interactions) is scored.

## Where the rules are enforced

- Pack briefs carry this list unchanged.
- An automatic check in `pack verify` and the pack builder that rejects cases breaking rules 1–4 is being added
  with the next pack builder. Until it lands, the review step below is the gate.
- Reviewers check every new pack against this page before it goes live.
